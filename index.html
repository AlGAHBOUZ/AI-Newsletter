How it works

From raw internet to your inbox

1

Collection

We scan official AI company blogs, Hacker News, and curated YouTube channels for anything published in the last 7 days.

2

AI analysis

Each piece is summarized, categorized, and scored for relevance by an LLM with a clear editorial brief: be specific, skip the hype, explain why it matters.

3

Filtering

Anything scoring below 5/10 is cut. Borderline pieces (5–6) are only kept if the summary has substance. You get the top 60–80% by default.

4

Delivery

A clean, well-structured HTML email lands in your inbox, readable in under 10 minutes, with every item linked back to its original source.
